### Hi, I'm Leonardo 👋

I'm a second-year Aerospace Engineering student at the **University of Bologna** (Forlì) and an aerodynamics specialist on the **Alma Rocketry** team.

I enjoy working at the intersection of **fluid dynamics and scientific computing** — from running high-fidelity CFD simulations to writing parallel PDE solvers and neural operators from scratch. My goal is to keep building faster, physics-grounded simulation tools and contribute to computational research projects.

### Projects

* **[`fourier-neural-operator-pde`](https://github.com/leonardomaddanu/fourier-neural-operator-pde)** — 2D Fourier Neural Operator in PyTorch for parametric Darcy flow with zero-shot super-resolution (`64² → 128²`).
* **[`mpi-2d-cartesian-solver`](https://github.com/leonardomaddanu/mpi-2d-cartesian-solver)** — Parallel finite-difference solver for the 2D heat equation built from scratch in C++ and OpenMPI.
* **[`pinn-lid-driven-cavity`](https://github.com/leonardomaddanu/pinn-lid-driven-cavity)** — Physics-Informed Neural Network solving the 2D incompressible Navier–Stokes equations in PyTorch.
* **[`microchannelcht`](https://github.com/leonardomaddanu/microchannelcht)** — 3D conjugate heat transfer simulation in OpenFOAM with three-grid GCI mesh verification.
* **[`nosecone-vk-cfd`](https://github.com/leonardomaddanu/nosecone-vk-cfd)** — Transonic Mach 0.9 aerodynamic simulation over a von Kármán nosecone in OpenFOAM.
